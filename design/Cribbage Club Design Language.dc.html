CC
Cribbage Club design language · v1.0

A serious game deserves a quiet room.

The competitive-cribbage design system: dark charcoal surfaces, one decisive green, gold reserved for skill. Data-dense, calm, fast. Full token source in Design Language.md.

01 · Principles

Five rules the whole product obeys

1
The count is always legible at a glance.
The pegging count is the largest live number on screen. Nothing may crowd, animate over, or restyle it mid-play.
2
Gold means skill, nothing else.
Rating gold appears only where ability is measured: ratings, trophies, rating deltas, the 121 hole. Never decoration, never buttons.
3
One green, and it always means "act."
Action green is the only saturated interactive color. If it's green, clicking it advances the game or the player.
4
Cards are instruments, not decoration.
Card faces are optimized for reading at 40px, not for beauty at 400px. No bevels, no gloss, no ornamental backs.
5
Records are content, motion is punctuation.
Ratings, streaks and analysis get layout priority everywhere. Animation confirms what happened in ≤320ms and gets out of the way — nothing bouncy, ever.

Replace gold-vs-white. Your color is the action green you already own everywhere in the UI — your pegs, your score popups, your bar accent. The opponent is card-stock ivory: neutral, never "premium." This frees gold for ratings (principle 2) and the ~9× luminance gap holds for all color-vision types.

05 · The board

Recommendation: flatten it — keep the warmth in the tint, not the texture

Drop the walnut canvas. A wood object floating in a charcoal UI reads as a different app — and it's exactly the skeuomorphism the brief bans. Instead the board becomes a first-class UI panel on a slightly warmer charcoal (--board #38342f), with drilled-hole affordances done in light and shadow, not material. It stays unmistakably a cribbage board while matching every other panel.
you · 14
opponent · 11
first 30 holes shown · gold-ringed hole = 121
Spec: board panel #38342f (2 steps warmer than surface-2), r12. Lanes are recessed tracks rgba(0,0,0,0.22). Holes 13px, fill #211f1d with inset top shadow — "drilled" via lighting only. Groups of 5 with 10px gaps; street markers 30/60/90 as caption labels below the track; hole 121 gets the only gold on the field: a 2px #e3aa24 ring. Pegs: front peg filled identity color + drop shadow, back peg 2px outlined ring of the same color. Mobile: the same lanes compress to a 6px linear track with peg dots — holes drop out below 480px width.

07 · Motion

Fast, monotonic, ≤320ms

Two easings only: --ease-out: cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1) for things arriving, --ease-in: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1) for things leaving. No spring, no overshoot, no bounce.

Card play (hand → table)180msease-outtranslate + ≤3° settle rotation
Starter cut (card flip)240msease-outrotateY, face legible by 60%
